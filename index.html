How we work

Small scope, clear ownership, steady delivery.

Decisions before tasks
Work stalls on unmade decisions far more often than on unfinished tasks. Every engagement starts by naming who decides what, and by when.
Status you can act on
Regular, honest reporting — including when something is off track. A status report that never says "no" is not a status report.
Structure that outlasts us
The goal is a team that runs the work without us. Handover is planned from the first week, not improvised at the end.
